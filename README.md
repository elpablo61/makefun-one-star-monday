One line: a one-star review of Monday as if it were a restaurant, 25 words or fewer.

> The server, Monday, took my order at 7:12, brought a 9 a.m. meeting instead of coffee, and billed my weekend as dessert.

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes that line to `/output/entry.txt` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt`), after checking it stays inside the gate: 1–180 characters and at most 25 words.
